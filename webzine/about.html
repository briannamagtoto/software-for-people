The webzine will emulate a PISONET machine. Friction will be a big part of this experience. The webzine will generally be about communal computing spaces with a particular focus on the PISONET, also commenting on general disparities within access to the internet. PISONETs, for Filipino youth, evolved into a third space (a place that was neither home nor school) and “turned internet browsing into a communal activity rather than a solitary one,” reflecting the Filipino tendency for “kapwa” or shared community.
